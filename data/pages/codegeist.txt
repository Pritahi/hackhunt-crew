Codegeist 2026: Build with Atlassian Forge and Rovo
$120,000+ in prizes · Online · Hosted by Atlassian on Devpost

About the challenge
Atlassian is calling on developers to build the next generation of teamwork
apps. Create a Forge app or a Rovo agent that helps teams know what is
decided, what is blocked, and what to do next.

ELIGIBILITY (from the Official Rules)
- Individuals who have reached the age of majority in their jurisdiction.
- Teams of eligible individuals.
- Employees of Atlassian and judges are not eligible.
- Residents of Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea,
  Syria, and the Crimea region are not eligible. India is NOT excluded.
- No purchase necessary. A free Atlassian Cloud developer instance and the
  Forge free tier are sufficient.

PRIZES (all cash, USD)
- Best Overall App: $15,000
- Category first places: multiple, $5,000-$10,000 each
- Bonus: Best Rovo Agent $1,500, plus additional bonus prizes
Total pool over $120,000.

KEY DATES
Submissions open: October 2026
Deadline: December 22, 2026, 12:00 PM Pacific Time
Judging: begins after the deadline
Winners announced: expected February 15, 2027

REQUIREMENTS
- A working Forge app or Rovo agent installed in an Atlassian Cloud instance
- A public demo video (under 5 minutes) showing the app; screen capture with
  captions is acceptable
- A public or shared repository with installation instructions
- A completed Devpost submission form

JUDGING
- Quality of the idea 33%
- Implementation 33%
- Potential impact 34%
