About
Build with Google Gemini invites builders across India to create production-
grade AI solutions on Gemini Enterprise. Solve one of four problem statements
around enterprise voice agents.

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE (from the official rules)
- Working professionals only. Students and non-professionals are not
  eligible.
- Participants must be based in India.
- Teams of 1-4 members.

PRIZES (total pool Rs 8,00,000)
Winner: Rs 3,00,000
1st Runner-up: Rs 2,00,000
2nd Runner-up: Rs 1,25,000
5 category prizes of Rs 35,000 each
Shortlisted teams receive $100 in Google Cloud credits for the prototype
phase.

PROCESS
1. Register and submit your idea by December 3, 2026.
2. Shortlisted teams build a prototype (deadline December 6).
3. Virtual Demo Day on December 18-19 with a 1-2 minute demo video.
4. LinkedIn build-in-public posts count for 10% of the score.

ENTRY FEE
None. Participation is free.

FAQ
