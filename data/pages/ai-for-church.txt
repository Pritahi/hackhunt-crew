About
The AI for Church community invites builders of all backgrounds to create AI
tools that help churches and ministries: sermon preparation helpers, outreach
assistants, administration copilots, translation tools, and more. Sponsored
credits from Lovable are available, but any stack is allowed.

ELIGIBILITY
- Open worldwide to anyone 18 or older.
- Individuals and teams welcome; solo entries are explicitly allowed.
- No purchase necessary. Free to enter.

PRIZES
Total cash pool: $2,500 USD, split among the top entries. The first-place
award is the largest share (announced as at least $1,000).
Winners are announced during a live online event on December 1, 2026.

SUBMISSION
- A working demo link or repository
- A short writeup of what the tool does and who it serves
- A 2-3 minute demo video (screen recording is fine)

JUDGING
- Impact for ministries 40%
- Execution 30%
- Creativity 30%

FAQ
