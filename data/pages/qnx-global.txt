QNX Global Hackathon · BlackBerry QNX · $50,000 in prizes · Online
Deadline: December 15, 2026, 2:00 PM EST

About
Build something remarkable on QNX 8.0, the real-time operating system inside
cars, robots, and medical devices. Use QEMU - no physical hardware required.

ELIGIBILITY (from the Official Rules)
- Individuals and teams who have reached the age of majority.
- Residents of Brazil, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, and North Korea are
  excluded. All other countries, including India, are eligible.
- No purchase necessary. QNX 8.0 is free for non-commercial use, and QEMU
  emulation is explicitly allowed instead of hardware.

PRIZES (all cash, USD)
Grand prize: $10,000
Five category first prizes: $4,000 each
Runner-up prizes: $2,000 each
Plus a Best Codelab award.

REQUIREMENTS
- A working project built on QNX 8.0 (QEMU images are fine)
- A public demo video
- A public repository with build and run instructions
- A completed Devpost submission

JUDGING
- Technical achievement 40%
- Innovation 30%
- Documentation and presentation 30%

FAQ
Do I need QNX hardware? No, QEMU is fully supported.
Can I enter alone? Yes.
