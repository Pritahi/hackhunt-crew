Build the future of QA automation with Kane CLI
Nov 12 - Nov 19, 2026 · 100% online · Free entry

Kane CLI is the command-line test runner for agentic QA. In this online
hackathon you will build a project that uses Kane CLI in a creative,
technically impressive way. Judges want to see real closed-loop automation:
an agent that writes or maintains test flows, runs them with Kane CLI, reads
the failures, and iterates.

ELIGIBILITY
The hackathon is open worldwide to individuals and teams of up to 4. You must
be 18 or older. Students, professionals, and independent builders are all
welcome. No purchase is necessary to enter or win. Void where prohibited.

Your repository must be initialized on or after November 12, 2026. Pre-existing
projects are not eligible.

PRIZES
1st place: $1,500 USD (cash, paid by bank transfer or PayPal)
2nd place: $750 USD
3rd place: $250 USD
Plus Kane CLI swag for the top 10 teams.

SCHEDULE (all times IST)

SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
- A public GitHub repository initialized on/after Nov 12, 2026
- A 3-minute screen recording demonstrating your project (no camera required;
  screen capture with captions or synthesized narration is fine)
- A completed submission form before the deadline

JUDGING CRITERIA
- Technical execution 35%
- Creativity 25%
- Use of Kane CLI 25%
- Presentation 15%

FAQ
Can I participate alone? Yes, teams of 1-4.
Is it free? Yes, completely.
Do I need special hardware? No.
Can I use any programming language? Yes, as long as Kane CLI runs the tests.
