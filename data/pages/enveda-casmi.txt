Enveda CASMI 2026: Molecule Identification from Mass Spectra
Kaggle · Featured Code Competition · $50,000 prize pool

Overview
Given tandem mass spectrometry (MS/MS) data, rank candidate molecular
structures (SMILES) for each spectrum. Submissions are scored by mean
reciprocal rank over 25 ranked candidates per molecule.
